## Essence, and what it is not
A loud postmodern print: sugary pastels and black, every surface its own pattern, the background a party of squiggles, zigzags, triangles and dots thrown around at odd angles.

Not crayon (printed, crisp patterns, black squiggles), not bauhaus (pastels and playful clutter, no strict primaries), not flat (patterns on every area, a busy stage).

## Shapes and line
A medium black outline on every part.

## Colour
Areas snap to pink, mint, yellow, lilac or cyan. The body stays flat; the belly gets black dots and the accent black stripes.

## Eyes and face
Solid black.

## Motion

## Pitfalls
- A busy stage can swallow the character: the stage stays at the edges.
- Patterned areas behind the face are kept off the body.
