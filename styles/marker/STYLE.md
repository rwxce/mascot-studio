## Essence, and what it is not
A quick drawing in a meeting room: chisel-tip markers in black, blue, red and green, a bold contour, areas filled with side-by-side strokes that darken where they overlap, faint ghosts of what was wiped off before.

Not crayon (smooth wet ink, streaks not wax, a white board), not line-art (thick line, coloured fills), not ink (four colours, streaky fills, boil).

## Shapes and line
A thick black marker contour that wobbles slightly. Areas are filled with broad parallel strokes that leave thin darker seams.

## Colour
Only four marker colours and the board's white. Light areas stay white.

## Eyes and face
Solid black marker.

## Motion
On twos, with a small boil on the line.

## Pitfalls
- Characters whose colours all snap to one marker read as one colour: the contour separates the parts.
- Erased ghosts stay faint and away from the face.
