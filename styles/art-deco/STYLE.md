# Art Deco

## Essence, and what it is not
A 1920s lobby panel: lacquer black, gold inlay, a rising fan of rays and stepped arcs, every line ruled and every shape mirrored.

Not gilded (black lacquer and machine geometry, no vellum, no diaper), not neon (solid black parts edged in metal, no glow), not stained glass (no colour, no lead).

## Shapes and line
No dark outline: every part is edged with a thin gold line, like inlay set into lacquer.

## Colour
Bodies are black lacquer with fine gold pinstripes; bellies cream; accents, props and lines polished gold.

## Eyes and face
Pale gold, so they shine out of the black.

## Motion
Smooth.

## Pitfalls
- A black body on a black page relies on the gold edge: never drop it.
- The fan must stay symmetric about the character's centre.
