## Essence, and what it is not
A small oil sketch painted wet into wet: every area is a crowd of short, loaded strokes with a light ridge on each, over a canvas you can still feel.

Not watercolor (opaque, thick, warm, no paper showing), not crayon (strokes are wide and ridged, not waxy scribbles), not clay (texture is the brush, not the thumb).

## Shapes and line
A dark umber contour painted with a loaded brush that wobbles a little. Inside, short curved strokes in three values of the area's colour.

## Colour
Every colour warmed towards ochre and pushed a touch more saturated. Eyes and line in burnt umber.

## Eyes and face
Solid umber, like a last dab of dark paint.

## Motion
Smooth, with a faint boil so the painted edge never sits perfectly still.

## Pitfalls
- Stroke patterns behind the eyes compete with the face: keep the stroke values close to the base colour.
- Very dark palettes lose the ridges: the highlight stroke must stay visible.
